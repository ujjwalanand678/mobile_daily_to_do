# Privacy Policy for Daily To-Do

**Last Updated: April 26, 2026**

Daily To-Do is committed to providing a secure and private productivity experience. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in our mobile application.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Daily To-Do is designed to be a **Privacy-First** application.
- **No Personal Data Collection**: We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal information (such as your name, email address, or contact details) to our own servers.
- **Local Storage**: All your tasks, notes, and productivity data are stored locally on your device using encrypted high-performance storage (MMKV).

## 2. Google Drive Synchronization
The App offers an optional synchronization feature using Google Drive.
- **Private App Data Folder**: If you choose to enable sync, the App uses the Google Drive `drive.appdata` scope. This creates a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that is inaccessible to other applications and invisible to you in your regular Drive view.
- **Data Ownership**: Your data is synced directly between your device and your personal Google Drive account. The developers of Daily To-Do never have access to your Google Drive or the data stored within it.

## 3. Permissions
The App may request the following permissions to function correctly:
- **Notifications**: To provide task reminders and Pomodoro timer alerts.
- **Internet Access**: Required solely for the purpose of syncing data with your Google Drive.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We use **Google Sign-In** solely for authentication and authorization to access your own Google Drive for syncing purposes. Please refer to [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for information on how they handle your data.

## 5. Security
We prioritize the security of your data by using native device security features and official Google APIs for cloud synchronization. Since your data is stored on your device and your private cloud, you maintain full control over its security.

## 6. Children’s Privacy
Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

## 8. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
